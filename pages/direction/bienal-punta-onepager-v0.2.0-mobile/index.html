One-pager

An institutional platform for three groups that rarely share a table: the capital of the Southern Cone, the institutions that regulate it and the companies that build its financial infrastructure. The first edition takes place in Punta del Este, Uruguay, in March 2027, over two days and in two venues, MACA and Casa Manantial.

    Capital. Rules.Infrastructure.One table.

    Every two years

    The agenda

    01
    01Stablecoins & PaymentsDollar stablecoins already carry a large share of the region’s digital-asset activity. The open questions are institutional: who may issue, who settles, what backs the reserves and which rules apply across borders.
    02RWA & TokenizationLand, credit, receivables and fund units on shared ledgers. Which assets move first, what legal certainty they need and how liquidity is created without weakening investor protection.
    03Agentic EconomySoftware agents are beginning to hold balances and make payments. How agentic payments are authorised, recorded and supervised, and who carries the liability.
