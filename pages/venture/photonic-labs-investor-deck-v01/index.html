02 — Unmet need
↘
5–8

Science is not the bottleneck. Scale-up is.

LATAM biotech reaches TRL 4 in public labs and stalls. Pilot production, GMP lots and a transferable dossier — TRL 5 to 8 — have no open provider in the region.

TRL 1–4
Covered

UdelaR, Institut Pasteur, INIA. University benches at near-zero marginal cost. Research gets done.

TRL 5–8
Empty

No open GMP‑ready pilot capacity between 10 and 300 L. No proportional downstream. No dossier a CDMO will accept.

TRL 9
Inaccessible

Industrial CDMOs with volume floors far above a startup's first lots.

Today founders ship molecules to India or Europe. US$50,000 per 100 g, verified with a Uruguayan first‑in‑class oncology startup. The alternative is the subsidized bench — which does not produce a pilot lot.

03 — Why now
↘

Four conditions aligned in 2025–26.

01Fiscal regime

Decreto 329/025 in force since February 2026: IRAE, wealth-tax and VAT exemptions on fixed assets; R&D counts ×1.5. Layered on Ley 16.462 (biotech = national interest) and the Zona Franca statute.

02Non-dilutive inside ZF

ANII instruments have been available to Zona Franca companies since 2025. The tax wrapper and the innovation agency no longer exclude each other — the same pairing that capitalized CPI in the UK.

03Capital next door

Argentina, Chile and Uruguay hold 89% of the region's biotech deals (LAVCA 2025). Uruguay's 62 biotech firms filed 60 patent applications in 2022–24, 85% from startups. The pipeline exists; the plant does not.

04Single-use maturity

10–300 L single‑use bioreactors and proportional downstream make a multi-client GMP‑ready plant buildable without stainless-steel CIP/SIP infrastructure: no cross-contamination between clients, turnaround in hours.
