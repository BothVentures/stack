Phases What each one has to produce

Six phases, each closed by a gate. A phase does not end because the calendar turns — it ends when its gate clears. F0 is nine days long and holds three of them.

F0 · Internal gateAug 12 → Aug 21

Decide whether this is a 2027 event at all

Nine days that determine everything after them. Three gates, one blocker, and a go/no-go that Gastón owns.

  • Financial model to Gastón — go/no-go. Four scenarios T0–T3: T0 at $200K in sponsorships is break-even with a ~$15K result; T1–T3 scale to $800K and up to $200K+. Same session carries the design and branding gate. Daiana + Rene → Gastón · Tue 12/08
  • Get the real MACA quote. Von Buch introduces us at the museum 14/08; cost check with Bob and Marco follows. This is the live blocker — the model cannot be validated against an estimated venue. Daiana + Rene
  • Budget gate ~$200K — the number that has to be authorized before branding and MVP spend starts.
  • Anchor prospect list drawn — a blockchain player or a regional bank. Search opens roughly two weeks after Gastón validates, so the list is built during F0, not after.
  • Wish list + Planning Beta continue from the draft — speaker Tier 1 mapping and production scope, both cheap to advance while the gate is pending.
  • Name and art direction staged for decision — four VB0.1 directions published, legal check with Andres on Punta Blockchain vs the registered Punta Crypto.

G1–G3Model validated · MACA cost confirmed · branding + MVP authorized (week of 19/08).

F1 · Branding + platformAug 24 → Oct 2

Build the thing you are going to sell

A short branding sprint, then six weeks of platform and deck work. Nothing goes public in this phase — it exists so that October has something real to announce.

  • Branding sprint — 7 to 10 days. One VB0.1 direction chosen and extended into the full system. Name locked before the sprint closes.
  • Sponsorship architecture built out — 21 positions across Master ($200K, 1 slot) · Diamond ($100K, 2) · Titanium (4) · Platinum · Supporter, on a $10K–$100K ladder. Top 7 slots present on both the MACA and Casa Manantial layers; the rest MACA only.
  • Three branded houses specified as sellable inventory — this is the part of the offer Blockchain.RIO does not sell, and the only justification for a $200K Master.
  • Documentación de Venta / deck system — main deck, forum deck, experience deck. The Rio package is the template, weighted our way.
  • Venta 1 opens — anchor search only. Warm approaches to one chain-level player or one regional bank. No public pricing.
  • Landing + MVP build toward the October go-live, plus a soft teaser hold at end of August.
  • MACA + Casa Manantial contracts — main-stage hold across Oct–Mar, dinner and party rooms specified.
  • Ticket-side pricing benchmark — the Rio decks carry no ticket pricing, so this half of the HIGH risk is still open. Close it here.

ExitBrand shipped, deck system complete, venues contracted, anchor conversations live.

F2 · Go live + anchorOct 5 → Oct 30

Go public, then cascade

The draft stacks website, announcement and PR into one fortnight. Sequence them — site, announcement, press — so each has something new to point at. Landing goes live before speakers are confirmed, by design.

  • Landing go-live + live announcement + PR push, in that order across the fortnight.
  • Anchor partner targeted and closed. Announce it, then sell Diamond and Titanium against a closed Master. The cascade only runs downward.
  • Venta 2 opens — full sell-down across the 21 positions, priced off the benchmarked ladder.
  • Speakers confirmation round — first names public, immediately after the site is live so the announcement has somewhere to land.
  • BCU President fireside confirmed on the record — the piece that makes the Friday forum unrepeatable elsewhere in the region.
  • Execution Phase 1 — production build, supplier contracts, hospitality design across both venues.

G4 · OctSite live and anchor signed. This is the commercial unlock for everything after.

F3 · Sell-down + ticketsNov 2 → Dec 18

Turn the announcement into revenue

Seven weeks of pure commercial work against a known inventory. Report positions closed, not meetings held.

  • Tickets open in November — seeding strategy first, then early bird. Tickets are secondary revenue by design; they build the room, sponsors build the P&L.
  • Sell-down tracked against the scenario ladder — which of T0/T1/T2/T3 the close rate is actually tracking toward, reported weekly, not at quarter end.
  • Master-unsold survivability held as an explicit scenario. If the P&L only works with a $200K Master, the venture has rebuilt the single-heroic-price problem it already rejected once — moved from tickets to sponsorship.
  • Closed-door regulatory roundtable — agenda and invitation list built now. Regulators need lead time that sponsors do not.
  • Execution Phase 2 — Preproducción opens 11/30 per the draft and runs through February.

G5 · NovTickets live and sell-down at or above the T1 line.

F4 · Program lockJan 4 → Feb 26, 2027

Close the content, then close the room

Two months where the product stops changing. Logistics against a moving agenda is how premium experiences fail.

  • Agenda closed + full lineup announced. After this, no new sessions.
  • Venta 3 — Supporter and local last-call positions; branded-house slots that are still open get repriced or retired, not discounted quietly.
  • Ticket push #2 plus accredited press and media partners.
  • Speaker briefings, roundtable prep with the regulators, and protocol logistics for the institutional opening.
  • Preproduction across both venues — AV, staging, F&B, transfers, accommodation blocks. March was chosen partly to dodge the January–February accommodation squeeze; hold that advantage by booking early.
  • Intelligence capture designed now — attendee and deal-flow data architected to feed BOTH's portfolio system, not only the event CRM.

G6 · JanAgenda and speakers closed. Production freezes against a fixed program.

F5 · ExecutionMar 1 → Mar 26, 2027

Two days, two layers

MACA carries the institutional weight; Casa Manantial carries the money moments. The rent linkage stops being an accounting note and becomes the shape of the event.

  • Friday — Leaders Forum. MACA auditorium, 100–150. Controlled institutional format: opening, BCU fireside on the record, closed-door regulatory roundtable. Private dinner 60–80 at Casa Manantial.
  • Saturday — Institutional Summit. MACA pabellones, 400–600 curated. Keynote, anchor panel, the branded houses live. Closing party 150–200 at Casa Manantial.
  • The venue is the argument. A museum for the institutional layer and our own house for the experience layer is the whole positioning — protect the contrast in production, don't blur the two into one event.
  • Post-event close — P&L against the scenario that was actually sold, Casa Manantial rent transfer, Year 2 renewals opened while the room is still warm.
  • Governance resolves here. The Rene / Daiana split was deliberately deferred to post-event; it comes back on the table with the P&L.
