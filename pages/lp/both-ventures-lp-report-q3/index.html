3.3

Reshore — working capital and data for North American manufacturers.

StageBuild · subscription
Real-asset infrastructure
Preview See the Reshore site → attached to this document

Working capital and operating tools for manufacturers in Mexico, the United States and Canada. Reshore finances a factory's cycle from verified purchase order to buyer payment (Advanced Factoring), and reads the operating data each order creates to price that capital and to run the plant's financial workflows (Reshore IQ). One agreement, one ledger: the cash and the data are underwritten in the same place.

Q3 trajectory. In Q2 Reshore was closing its round with the product still being defined. In Q3 it found its positioning and value proposition: capital as the wedge into the factory, data as the asset that compounds. The offer is now specified end to end. Advanced Factoring advances funds against a verified purchase order to cover production, and is repaid when the buyer pays, on cycles of up to 120 days; the rate improves as the factory connects its operating data — production, tooling, quality, materials and operations records, up to 65 data types. Reshore IQ comes with the capital: it reads purchase orders, invoices and payments into the factory's ERP and shows efficiency gains, operational risks and opportunities to improve; a factory can also connect to it before using Reshore's capital. Eligibility is set — manufacturers in North America with buyer contracts of one year or more, recurring orders and a predictable payment cycle. The public site is built and in final review.

Round closed. In Q2 we reported a USD 400,000 round open at a USD 12M–15M cap, with BOTH's USD 200,000 committed for July. It closed in Q3 at a USD 12M cap: USD 200,000 from BOTH, instrumented as a SAFE, and USD 200,000 from a co-investor. BOTH's cash deployment in Reshore now totals USD 400,000.

Financing stack. Reshore received an engagement letter from JPMorgan for a USD 25M facility with the US International Development Finance Corporation (DFC), oriented to the pre-shipment advance that Advanced Factoring makes at the verified purchase order. On the Mexican side, a guarantee route through Bancomext (Mexico's export-development bank) is open.

The data layer. Every enrolled factory's operating data is structured, encrypted and anonymized record by record in Reshore's data vault, and stays the factory's. Inside the financing business it lowers the cost of capital and opens financing earlier — at the verified purchase order, before shipment. Beyond it, the same corpus — years of real operations across North American plants — is the asset Reshore intends to license to AI labs training models for the physical world (robotics and automation), under separate data-partnership agreements.

Next step. With the product specified and the facility in process, Reshore moves to its next capital event: an equity raise of USD 5M at a USD 20M pre-money valuation, alongside a USD 10M debt facility to fund the first advances.

EndgameThe working-capital and data partner for North American manufacturers — Advanced Factoring from purchase order to payment, Reshore IQ in every enrolled plant, and a structured, anonymized operating-data corpus licensed beyond the financing business. Scales on third-party capital.
